# Farfield Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Farfield is a Chrome extension that helps
teams see and reduce the corrections people make to AI output.

## What the extension collects
When you use supported sites (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini,
Copilot, and Gmail), Farfield records:
- Follow-up prompts you send after an AI response (re-prompts)
- Which AI tool and page they were sent on, and timestamps
- Content of files you manually upload for comparison
  (e.g. Excel or PowerPoint versions)

## Where data is stored
All collected data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's
storage. Farfield does not currently operate a server and does not
transmit this data to us or to any third party.

## How data is used
Data is used only to show you patterns, proposed fixes, and results
inside the Farfield dashboard on your device.

## Sharing and selling
We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We do not use it for
advertising, credit decisions, or any purpose unrelated to the
extension's single purpose.

## Your control
You can view, export, or delete all stored data from the dashboard's
Data view, or by removing the extension, which deletes its local data.

## Changes
If we add a server-backed or team feature, we will update this policy
before data leaves your device.
